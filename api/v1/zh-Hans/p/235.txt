请使用「漏斗图（Funnel chart）」，它是「资料中常用的图解与图表名称」中的一个名称。
Motion Prompt #235

这是什么：人数逐级减少的漏斗
示例画面：四根居中的条自上而下依次从中部展开，一根比一根短，之间以浅灰色梯形相连。
规格：
- Bars: 470, 350, 236 and 132 px wide, 34 px tall, 10 px apart, 7 px corners; light blue, mid blue, mid blue, dark blue
- Motion: from the top, 0.36 s apart starting at 0.4 s, each bar grows out from the middle over 0.45 s (ease-out)
- Joints: light gray trapezoids (opacity 0.8) narrowing from the bar above to the bar below fill the gaps
- Numbers: 100, 58, 27 and 9 in the middle of the bars (20 px), fading in over 0.25 s from 0.3 s after each bar starts
- The bars are separate and centered so they do not read as #220 Pyramid diagram upside down
- End: the whole figure fades out from 4.1 s to 4.5 s and the card stays empty until the 4.7 s loop
适用场景：To show how the count drops at each stage, such as visit → sign-up → purchase.
