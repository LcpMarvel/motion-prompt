Motion Prompt #228

这是什么：用蛛网的形状比较多个项目
示例画面：六边形蛛网上，灰色多边形自中心展开，随后蓝色多边形略带回弹地展开。
规格：
- Scale: four nested hexagons up to radius 86 px (the outer one darker) and six spokes; they stay for the whole cycle
- Comparison: a gray polygon (values 0.46–0.74, fill opacity 0.22) spreads out from the center from 0.4 s to 1.1 s (ease-out)
- Main: a blue polygon (values 0.92, 0.58, 0.84, 0.4, 0.76, 0.52; fill opacity 0.28, 3 px outline, dots on the corners) spreads from 0.95 s to 1.85 s, a 55:45 blend of ease-out and ease-out-back for a slight overshoot
- End: the axes and grid stay; only the data fades out from 4.15 s to 4.55 s (one pass = 4.7 s)
适用场景：To compare the balance of several items, or the strengths and weaknesses of two things.
