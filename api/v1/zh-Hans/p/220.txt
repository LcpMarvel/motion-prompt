Motion Prompt #220

这是什么：由下往上叠起的层级
示例画面：四层三角形自下而上堆叠，每层依次落位；只有顶层是深蓝色。
规格：
- Shape: a triangle from y = 16 (apex) to y = 192 (base), 336 px wide at the base, cut into four layers with 6 px gaps
- Colors from the bottom: light gray, light blue, mid blue, dark blue
- Motion: from the bottom layer up, 0.32 s apart (0.4, 0.72, 1.04, 1.36 s), each layer drops 34 px into place over 0.4 s while fading in (ease-out)
- End: the whole figure fades out from 4.1 s to 4.5 s and the card stays empty until the 4.7 s loop
适用场景：To show stages that build on each other, or levels that get smaller toward the top.
