请使用「象形图（图标阵列）（Pictogram chart (icon array)）」，它是「资料中常用的图解与图表名称」中的一个名称。
Motion Prompt #231

这是什么：用人或物的图标数量表示比例
示例画面：十个人形图标以灰色排成一行，前三个逐个变蓝，「30%」同步递增计数。
规格：
- People: ten faceless person icons, 80 px tall and 32 px apart, in a single row (a grid would read as a heatmap)
- Entrance: they pop in from 0.35 s, 0.06 s apart, over 0.3 s each (ease-out-back), all gray
- Color: the first three turn from gray to blue over 0.25 s each, from 1.25 s, 0.22 s apart
- Number: "30%" (46 px, blue) on the left counts up from 0 between 1.25 s and 1.94 s, with "10人中3人" (3 in 10 people) below
- End: the axes and grid stay; only the data fades out from 4.15 s to 4.55 s (one pass = 4.7 s)
适用场景：To make a share feel like a count of people, for readers who do not like numbers.
