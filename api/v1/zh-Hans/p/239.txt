Motion Prompt #239

这是什么：横向排列箭头形状的步骤
示例画面：箭头形的步骤 1–5 从左依次滑入，各带简短说明；蓝色向右逐渐加深。
规格：
- Shape: five chevrons 104 px wide and 74 px tall (point depth 24 px, 6 px gaps); from the second on, the left side is notched to fit the one before
- Colors: light blue, light blue, mid blue, mid blue, dark blue, with the numbers 1–5 (24 px) in the middle
- Motion: from 0.4 s, 0.3 s apart, each slides in from 34 px to the left over 0.42 s while fading in (ease-out)
- Notes: two gray bars (62 px and 44 px long) under each step, fading in over 0.3 s from 0.25 s after the step starts
- End: the whole figure fades out from 4.1 s to 4.5 s and the card stays empty until the 4.7 s loop
适用场景：To show steps that follow each other, such as a training schedule or a rollout, in one row.
