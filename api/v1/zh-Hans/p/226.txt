Motion Prompt #226

这是什么：用圆的大小表示第三个数字
示例画面：L 形轴上五个大小不一的半透明圆依次长大；只有最大的一个是深蓝色。
规格：
- Axes: the same L-shaped axes and grid lines as #225 Scatter plot; they stay for the whole cycle
- Circles: five, radius 14–44 px, semi-transparent fill (opacity 0.5; the largest is dark blue at 0.42) with a 2.5 px outline in the same color
- Motion: from 0.45 s, 0.24 s apart, each grows over 0.5 s with a small overshoot (ease-out-back)
- Unlike #225 Scatter plot: only a few points, and the circle size carries a third number
- End: the axes and grid stay; only the data fades out from 4.15 s to 4.55 s (one pass = 4.7 s)
适用场景：To compare three numbers at once: across, up and size.
